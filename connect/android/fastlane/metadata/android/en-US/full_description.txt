PeerPayload Connect sends files and folders straight from one device to another: phone to PC, PC to phone, on the same network or across networks.

<b>No account</b>
<ul>
<li>Each device makes its own key the first time it opens. No sign-up, email or phone number.</li>
<li>Pair a device once with a QR code or a one-time link, and compare the safety code both screens show.</li>
<li>Only paired devices can send you anything, and every offer needs your Accept.</li>
</ul>

<b>Works wherever your devices are</b>
<ul>
<li>Each transfer tries a direct connection, then has the receiver connect out to the sender, then falls back to a relay.</li>
<li>All three use mutual TLS between the two devices, each pinning the other's key. The relay only passes encrypted bytes.</li>
<li>The mailbox and relay servers are open source; you can run your own and set them in Settings.</li>
</ul>

<b>Built for big files</b>
<ul>
<li>Every chunk is checked with SHA-256.</li>
<li>Pause and resume from either side, even after a restart.</li>
<li>Share files from any app, with a review step before sending.</li>
<li>Transfers continue in the background with a progress notification.</li>
<li>Optional "Stay ready to receive": offers arrive with Accept/Decline even when the app is closed, without Google's push service.</li>
</ul>

Desktop apps for Windows and Linux are on the project page. Free and open source under the Apache License 2.0, with no ads, tracking or analytics.

Early preview: no independent security audit yet, and the free hosted servers can take up to a minute to wake after being idle.
